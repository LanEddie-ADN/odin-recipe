Pork Sisig

Description

Pork Sisig is a crispy, savory, and tangy Filipino dish made from chopped grilled pork belly mixed with onions, calamansi, and chili.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs pork belly
  • 1 large red onion, chopped
  • 3 chili peppers, chopped
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 4 pieces calamansi, juiced
  • 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Boil the pork belly in water for 40 minutes until tender, then drain.
  2. Grill or pan-fry the pork until the skin becomes crispy.
  3. Chop the crispy pork into small bite-sized pieces.
  4. Mix the pork in a bowl with onions, chili, soy sauce, calamansi juice, and mayonnaise.
  5. Serve hot on a plate or sizzling pan.
