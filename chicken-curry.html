Filipino Chicken Curry

Description

Filipino Chicken Curry is a creamy comfort dish made with chicken simmered in coconut milk, curry powder, and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken pieces
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 2 tablespoons curry powder
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 onion and 4 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce

Steps

  1. Fry the potatoes and carrots in a pan for 2 minutes, then set them aside.
  2. In the same pot, fry the garlic and onion until soft.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Add the fish sauce, curry powder, and coconut milk, then simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. Add the potatoes, carrots, and bell pepper, and cook for 10 more minutes until soft.
